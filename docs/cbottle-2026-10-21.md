# The cBottle attempt, 2026-10-20 to 21: every number, for a later post-mortem

The first full sweep flew on the released cBottle-video winds and was abandoned the next morning: with only
two levels in the balloon's band, both at jet speeds, no controller could hold position, and the experiment
measured drift. The wind source moved to a global ERA5 record (DESIGN.md, 32). Everything measured on cBottle
is kept here and on Unicorn so the decision can be revisited.

## The records

Released cBottle-video rollouts (NVIDIA pretrained weights, no retraining), 14 days at 6 h (56 frames), global
HEALPix nside 64, converted by `scripts/convert_cbottle.py` to a 2 degree grid from 80S to 80N at the 200 and
50 hPa levels (11.8 and 20.6 km), the only two of its eight pressure levels anywhere near the balloon band
(the rest: 1000, 850, 700, 500, 300 hPa below, 10 hPa = 31 km above). Raw rollouts in
`~/cbottle-weights/outputs/video_{jan,jul}_s{100,200}_*/0.nc` on Unicorn; converted records in
`~/balloon-records/cbottle_{jan,jul}_s{100,200}.npz` (12 MB each).

## One flight, traced (seed 0, 300 km downwind goal, hindsight)

Start 26.1S 37.5E at 16.2 km, goal 27.2S 40.3E (the balloon is 83 km from it after one move). The calmest
blend at the start is 3.9 m/s at 20.6 km (33.5 m/s at 11.8 km, 17.8 m/s in the middle). It still drifted:
1,344 km away by move 5, 2,940 by move 10, 4,661 by move 20, ending 1,330 km away; average distance 2,981 km.
Plans compared at the start, cost summed over 50 moves: climb to the top and hold 197,598; coast in the middle
557,853; best of 3,000 random plans 182,024; L-BFGS 100 iterations 152,729; L-BFGS 500 iterations 409,797
(the refinement wandered uphill and was kept: the bug fixed in 233ef16); normalised descent 197,598. After the
fix, 100 and 500 iterations both give 152,7xx. Not the optimiser, then: the best plan anyone found still averages
3,000 km away.

## Why: the altitude band

The balloon flies 14 to 21 km; released cBottle outputs 11.8 and 20.6 km and nothing between. The simulator
blends those two linearly, so the only "calm" air is where two opposed jets happen to cancel, and that place
moves. On ERA5's six levels across the band a calm level under 5 m/s exists at 70% of points, and hindsight holds
a 300 km goal to a median 200 km (North Pacific box) or 573 km (global, within 60 degrees of the equator; the
bad seeds launched into the southern winter jet at 35 m/s). Shaurya's retrained two-stage generator (18 levels,
14.3 to 20.2 km) would not have this problem once time-stitching exists.
