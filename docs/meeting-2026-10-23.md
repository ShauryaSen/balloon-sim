## Results (figures in `figures/1000km/`)

1. `horizons_summed_km_n0.png`: **the breaking point**. A longer horizon helps up to 60 h with a good forecast;
   between 3.2 and 6.4 m/s of forecast error (1 to 2 times a real day-old forecast) the horizons converge, and
   no horizon recovers performance after that. Same break on the 300 km task.
2. `lines_summed_km_h20.png`: hindsight below MPC below the fixed plan at every noise level; replanning saves
   about 400 km of average distance once the error passes 3 m/s.
3. `score_summed_km_h20.png`: MPC closes 60 to 70% of the gap between a blind plan and hindsight, falling to 20%
   at the highest noise.
4. `flight_seed3_sigma3.19.png`: one flight, to make it concrete.
5. Sampling belief draws barely helps MPC (2 of 24 comparisons, at the highest noise); a trend for the fixed plan.
