# Results, 2026-10-22: the noise sweep with a 1,000 km goal

The third sweep. The goal moved from 300 to 1,000 km downwind by a rule fixed before testing
(`goal-distance-test-2026-10-22.md`). Everything else is as in `results-2026-10-21.md` (the 300 km sweep).
Rows on Unicorn in `~/balloon-sim/results/rows/` (300 km in `results-300km/`); figures in `figures/1000km/`
(gitignored, regenerate with `uv run scripts/plot.py <rows> --into figures/1000km`); tables from
`scripts/summarise.py`. Slurm arrays 471930-471932, 471939 and 471940 (the 64-draw cells, at 40 GB). All 4,064
cells, no errors.

## Setup

Global ERA5, 2021-07-16, 3 h moves, six levels 14.8 to 20.2 km. Launches 20N to 50N at the middle altitude, goal
1,000 km downwind, 50 moves (6.25 days). Belief: GP around the forecast, kernel fitted in the band to a day-old
forecast's error (3.2 m/s, 544 km, 1.3 km, 9.1 h), conditioned on the balloon's own readings. Noise sigma =
{0, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4} x 3.2 m/s. Hindsight is the best search found on the true wind, per seed and metric
(DESIGN.md 36). 32 paired seeds.

Knobs, after the Tuning Playbook: **scientific** sigma, MPC horizon, belief draws, controller. **Fixed** goal
distance and direction (rule-chosen), launch band (summer mid-latitudes), episode length, climb cap, kernel
(fitted), planner settings (Myles's defaults, L-BFGS). **Nuisance** seed (paired, 32). Not ablated and likely to
interact with the scientific variables: the kernel's calibration (noise and belief share it by construction),
episode length, and the launch band.

## Hindsight, the ceiling

Closest approach a median 4 km (quartiles 0 to 43), within 200 km on 29 of 32 seeds, at a median move of 26 of 50.
Final distance a median 262 km (94 to 783); average distance over the episode 310 km (236 to 709). The goal is
reached about halfway through and then lost to drift, as with 300 km, but the first half is now steering toward it.

## Findings (each a paired 95% interval over 32 seeds; "not detectable" means the interval contains zero)

1. **Order holds everywhere.** Fixed plan above MPC above hindsight at every noise level, every horizon, every
   draw count; every excess over hindsight is positive.
2. **Replanning pays once the forecast is wrong.** MPC (horizon 20, mean) beats the fixed plan by 171 km at
   sigma 1.6 [30, 312], 381 at 3.2 [196, 566], 392 at 6.4 [223, 560], 397 at 12.8 [176, 617], on 23 to 30 of 32
   seeds. Not detectable at sigma 0 or 0.8, where there is little to correct.
3. **The breaking point (C3).** With a good forecast, a longer horizon pays up to 20 moves (60 h): horizon 10 to 20
   helps at sigma 0, 1.6 and 3.2 (by about 100 km), and 20 to 50 is never detectably better below sigma 12.8. From
   sigma 6.4 on, 10 to 20 no longer helps: the horizons converge (figure `horizons_summed_km_n0.png`). So a longer
   horizon stops recovering performance between sigma 3.2 and 6.4 m/s, one to two times a day-old forecast's
   error. The 300 km sweep put the same break at the same place.
4. **Draws.** For MPC, 16 draws against the mean are within seed noise in 22 of 24 comparisons; the two exceptions
   are horizon 20 at the two highest noise levels (91 km [21, 161] and 135 km [14, 257] better). With 24 tests at
   95%, one or two would be expected by chance, so this is suggestive, not established. For the fixed plan, 64
   draws trend better up to sigma 3.2 (by up to 134 km) but no interval excludes zero.
5. **Score.** MPC at horizon 20 closes a median 0.58 to 0.70 of the gap between the fixed plan and hindsight up to
   sigma 3.2, then 0.53 at 6.4 and 0.22 at 12.8. Unlike the 300 km task, where it held near 0.5, the share falls at
   high noise: with a far goal, a bad forecast eventually costs MPC as much as it costs the blind plan.

## Against the 300 km sweep

Same story, clearer: the order, the benefit of replanning, and the break between sigma 3.2 and 6.4 all repeat. The
1,000 km goal gives hindsight a closer approach (4 km against 47 km median), keeps the final distance nearer
(262 km against about 470 km), and separates the horizons more cleanly. One flight, drawn by
`scripts/map_flight.py`, is in `figures/1000km/flight_seed3_sigma3.19.png`.

## Caveats

- Hindsight is the best our optimisers found on the true wind, not the true optimum.
- The goal is reached, then lost to drift; holding it would need a station-keeping cost.
- The belief is calibrated by construction; a mismatched belief is untested.
- One ERA5 window (2021-07-16, ten days) and one season. Results are about this window until another is run.
- Within a seed the noise is one error pattern scaled by sigma, not independent draws per level.
